# Code of Conduct Adoption Plan

Before public community launch, adopt a recognized current Contributor Covenant text without rewriting its behavioral standard. Preserve its required attribution and link to the exact upstream version.

The canonical root `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` is blocked until the Creator approves:

- the version adopted;
- the enforcement contact/team;
- the scope of project spaces and off-platform representation;
- the moderation/escalation process;
- record access and retention responsibilities.

Prefer a role mailbox or repository-host reporting mechanism staffed by actual maintainers. Do not invent a contact, copy the inherited repository's moderation identity, or publish an enforcement promise no one owns.

Once approved, add the canonical file, link it from `CONTRIBUTING.md` and community health files, test links, and ensure issue/discussion templates route conduct reports privately rather than requesting public details.
